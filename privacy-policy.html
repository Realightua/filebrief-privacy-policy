Filebrief

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Filebrief Meta application “Filebrief Deal Inbox” (the “App”) and related Filebrief Host software operated for a customer firm (the “Firm”). The Firm’s designated operator controls the Filebrief Host instance that receives and stores WhatsApp Business messages.

2. What this App does

Filebrief is an internal deal / document assistant. The WhatsApp integration lets authorized Firm staff forward business messages and files to the Firm’s WhatsApp Business number. Those messages are delivered by Meta’s WhatsApp Cloud API to the Firm’s Filebrief Host, where they appear in the Firm’s Emails & Messages inbox for deal work.

This is not a consumer social app. It is intended for Firm staff and the Firm’s own WhatsApp Business account, not for public end-user signup.

3. Data we process

Depending on how the Firm configures the App, processing may include:

  • WhatsApp message content (text and, in later features, media metadata/files)
  • Sender phone numbers (E.164) and WhatsApp profile display names
  • Message identifiers, timestamps, and delivery/webhook metadata from Meta
  • Staff allowlist entries linking phone numbers to Firm users/roles
  • Technical logs needed to operate and secure the webhook (for example signature validation results)

Meta Platforms, Inc. processes WhatsApp traffic as the messaging platform provider under Meta’s own terms and policies while messages transit WhatsApp / Cloud API.

4. How we use data

  • To ingest inbound WhatsApp messages into the Firm’s Filebrief Host inbox
  • To enforce staff allowlisting and security controls (signature checks, audit events)
  • To support deal filing, search, and related Firm workflows inside Filebrief
  • To operate, troubleshoot, and secure the integration

We do not sell personal data. We do not use WhatsApp content for advertising.

5. Where data is stored

Message content accepted by the integration is stored on the Firm’s Filebrief Host (on-premises or firm-controlled appliance), not in a Filebrief multi-tenant public cloud inbox. Meta may retain WhatsApp platform data according to Meta’s policies.

6. Sharing

Data may be shared only with:

  • Meta / WhatsApp, as required to provide Cloud API messaging
  • The Firm’s authorized users and administrators of that Filebrief Host
  • Service providers the Firm engages to host or support its own infrastructure, under the Firm’s contracts
  • Authorities when required by law

7. Retention

Retention follows the Firm’s Filebrief Host configuration and internal policies (including any legal hold or export rules the Firm enables). Webhook raw payloads may be retained temporarily for replay, audit, and troubleshooting.

8. Security

In transit to the Host, Meta webhooks are authenticated (for example HMAC signature verification). Access inside Filebrief is limited to authenticated Firm users under the Host’s access controls. No security measure is perfect; the Firm is responsible for securing its Host, network, and accounts.

9. Your choices and rights

Staff can stop forwarding messages to the business number. Firm administrators can remove phones from the allowlist and manage retention on the Host. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data. To exercise rights relating to data on a Filebrief Host, contact the Firm that operates that Host. For WhatsApp platform account matters, use Meta/WhatsApp in-product controls and Meta’s privacy channels.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 16 and is not intended for consumer child use.

11. International transfers

WhatsApp / Meta may process data in regions where Meta operates. Firm Host storage location depends on where the Firm deploys Filebrief.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy where permitted by law.

13. Contact

Privacy questions about this Meta App or Filebrief documentation: open an issue on github.com/Realightua/filebrief-privacy-policy.

For data stored on a specific Firm’s Host, contact that Firm’s Filebrief administrator.
